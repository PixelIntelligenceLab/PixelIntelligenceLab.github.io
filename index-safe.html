Where we focus

Imaging domains where we have deep technical grounding and where the clinical bottleneck is well understood. These are areas of active engagement and development, not cleared products.

  • Capsule endoscopy In development

    Reading assistance for capsule studies

    A single capsule study runs to tens of thousands of frames and hours of expert reading. We develop triage approaches that rank frames by clinical significance and abstain rather than guess when confidence is low, with evaluation designed around performance on data the model has never seen.

    Relevant to GI practices, capsule device manufacturers and reading services.

  • Colonoscopy In development

    Polyp detection and segmentation at video rates

    Real-time detection and delineation built on openly published architectures and benchmarks. Our emphasis is on how these models behave across centers and scope generations, and on measuring them against the outcome clinicians care about — adenoma detection rate — rather than a single held-out split.

    Relevant to endoscopy tower and scope manufacturers, and ambulatory surgery centers.

  • Liver MRI In development

    Non-invasive liver assessment

    Volumetric segmentation and staging of chronic liver disease from multiparametric MRI, with calibrated uncertainty so borderline studies are flagged instead of silently scored. Much of the practical work is the pipeline around the model: DICOM in, structured report out.

    Relevant to radiology groups, hepatology clinics and imaging endpoints in trials.

  • Dental radiography Exploratory

    Structured reading of panoramic and periapical images

    Vision-language approaches that answer clinical questions about a radiograph and draft structured findings. The target is the volume problem in general dentistry: many images, thin radiology support, and wide variance between readers.

    Relevant to dental service organizations and dental imaging software vendors.

  • Low-dose CT Exploratory

    Diagnostic quality at reduced dose

    Generative denoising for low-dose CT, assessed by task-based detectability rather than PSNR alone, and designed to fit existing reconstruction pipelines without retraining on site data.

    Relevant to CT manufacturers and imaging centers under dose-reduction mandates.

  • Digital pathology Exploratory

    Classification without a labeled training set

    Retrieval-based classification over frozen self-supervised features with confidence-filtered abstention: a lab supplies a small reference gallery instead of thousands of annotated slides. This removes the annotation bottleneck that stalls most pathology pilots before they start.

    Relevant to pathology labs, contract research organizations and biobanks.

  • Validation service Available now

    Generalization audit

    Independent stress-testing of your model, not ours. Cross-scanner, cross-site and cross-device evaluation, plus model-selection stability analysis. Most teams have never seen their model's numbers on data from outside their own institution, and that gap is usually where deployments fail.

    Relevant to medtech teams preparing regulatory submissions or partner pilots.

How we work with teams

Six engagement types, scoped as independent consulting work. Most begin with a short paid scoping call rather than a proposal.

Independent model validation

We test your model on data it has never seen: cross-scanner, cross-site, cross-device, plus model-selection stability analysis. You get the numbers before a reviewer, a regulator or a customer finds them. Fixed-scope engagements with a written report.

Expert witness and litigation support

Prior art analysis, invalidity and infringement opinions, and expert testimony in medical imaging and machine learning disputes, grounded in a decade of peer-reviewed publication in the field. Hourly for analysis and reports; flat fee for deposition and trial.

Technical due diligence

For investors and acquirers evaluating medical imaging AI. We assess whether reported performance is real, whether it will hold outside the seller's data, and what it would take to reach a regulatory submission. Delivered as a written assessment within two weeks.

Dataset strategy

How much data, from how many sites, annotated by whom, to what protocol. Annotation guidelines, inter-reader analysis, and an honest answer on whether your dataset can support the claim you intend to make.

Advisory engagements

Ongoing scientific and technical advisory for healthcare AI teams — design review, literature and competitive assessment, and second opinions on what to build, what to license and what to stop. Retainer or advisory board seat.

Research strategy and proposal review

Technical review and strengthening of federal research proposals in medical imaging AI, including specific aims, approach, and evaluation design. Flat fee, billed on delivery — we do not work on contingency or success fees.
