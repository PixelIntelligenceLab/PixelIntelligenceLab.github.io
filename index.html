What we're building

Six programs moving from published research toward deployable clinical tools. Each one started with a bottleneck clinicians described to us, not a benchmark we wanted to win.

  1. Capsule endoscopy SBIR Phase I

    AIEndoScan — capsule reading assistant

    A single capsule study is tens of thousands of frames and hours of expert reading. AIEndoScan triages the video down to a ranked set of clinically significant frames — bleeding, ulcers, polyps, angiectasia — and abstains rather than guessing when confidence is low. Built on multi-center capsule data with out-of-distribution evaluation across devices and sites.

    For GI practices, capsule device manufacturers and reading services.

  2. Colonoscopy Multi-center validation

    Real-time polyp detection and segmentation

    Production-oriented descendants of ColonSegNet, ResUNet++, DoubleUNet and TransNetR, trained on PolypDB and Kvasir-SEG and tested across centers and scope generations. Measured against the outcome that matters clinically — adenoma detection rate — rather than Dice on a single held-out split.

    For endoscopy tower and scope manufacturers, and ambulatory surgery centers.

  3. Liver MRI Prototype

    LiverProfile AI — non-invasive liver assessment

    Volumetric segmentation and staging of chronic liver disease from multiparametric MRI, built on CirrMRI600+, with calibrated uncertainty so borderline studies are flagged instead of silently scored. Delivered through VoxPixel, our end-to-end deployment framework: DICOM in, structured report out.

    For radiology groups, hepatology clinics, and trials needing imaging endpoints.

  4. Dental imaging Dataset release

    DentiAsk — radiograph question answering

    A vision-language system for panoramic and periapical radiographs that answers clinical questions and drafts structured findings, backed by a purpose-built annotated dataset. Aimed at the volume problem in general dentistry: many images, thin radiology support, and wide variance between readers.

    For dental service organizations and dental imaging software vendors.

  5. Low-dose CT Research preview

    CTDenoiser — diagnostic quality at reduced dose

    One-step generative denoising for low-dose CT, benchmarked against established reconstruction and denoising baselines and scored by task-based detectability rather than PSNR alone. Designed to drop into existing reconstruction pipelines without retraining on site data.

    For CT manufacturers and imaging centers under dose-reduction mandates.

  6. Digital pathology Research preview

    Training-free tissue classification

    Retrieval-based classification over frozen self-supervised features with confidence-filtered abstention: a lab supplies a small reference gallery instead of thousands of annotated slides. Removes the annotation bottleneck that stalls most pathology AI pilots before they start.

    For pathology labs, contract research organizations and biobanks.

  7. Validation service Available now

    Generalization audit

    Independent stress-testing of your model, not ours. Cross-scanner, cross-site and cross-device evaluation plus model-selection stability analysis — the protocol we used to benchmark capsule endoscopy classifiers across three independent datasets. Most teams have never seen their model's numbers outside their own data.

    For medtech teams preparing regulatory submissions or partner pilots.

Research use only. None of the above are FDA cleared or intended for diagnostic use.

How we work

Four connected practices, built on a decade of peer-reviewed medical imaging research.

Curated clinical datasets

Multi-center, expertly annotated data for colonoscopy, capsule endoscopy and liver MRI — designed for generalizability, with documented annotation protocols and inter-reader analysis.

Segmentation and detection models

Medical image segmentation architectures spanning real-time polyp detection through transformer and state-space models for organ and lesion segmentation in CT and MRI.

Validation and benchmarking

Statistically grounded evaluation: out-of-distribution testing, cross-scanner generalization, uncertainty quantification, and diagnostic accuracy studies built toward regulatory-grade evidence.

Advisory and collaboration

Dataset strategy for medtech teams, technical advisory for healthcare AI groups, and academic–industry collaborations from grant-funded research to product-ready prototypes.

The gap worth closing is between what imaging AI achieves in a paper and what it delivers at the bedside.

Pixel Intelligence Lab grows out of a decade of work at the intersection of computer vision and clinical medicine. Its founder developed several of the most widely used open datasets and architectures in gastrointestinal imaging — including Kvasir-SEG, PolypDB, GastroVision and CirrMRI600+ — alongside segmentation models such as ResUNet++, DoubleUNet, ColonSegNet and TransNetR, with research published at MICCAI, IEEE TMI, ICASSP, MIDL and leading clinical journals.

Closing that gap means multi-center data, honest out-of-distribution evaluation, and models designed for the realities of clinical deployment — including the cases where the right answer is for the model to decline to answer.
