(0008,103E) SERIES DESCRIPTION — PROGRAMS

What we're building

Active programs translating our research into deployable clinical tools. Each one starts from a problem clinicians raised, not a benchmark.

01 · CAPSULE ENDOSCOPYSBIR PHASE I

AIEndoScan — capsule reading assistant

A single capsule study is tens of thousands of frames and hours of expert reading. AIEndoScan triages the video to a ranked set of clinically significant frames — bleeding, ulcers, polyps, angiectasia — and abstains rather than guessing on low-confidence findings. Built on multi-center capsule data with explicit out-of-distribution evaluation across devices and sites.

FOR: GI practices · capsule device makers · reading services

02 · COLONOSCOPYMULTI-CENTER VALIDATION

Real-time polyp detection & segmentation

Production-oriented descendants of ColonSegNet, ResUNet++, DoubleUNet and TransNetR, trained on PolypDB and Kvasir-SEG and tested across centers and scope generations. Optimized against the outcome that matters clinically — adenoma detection rate — not Dice on a single held-out split.

FOR: endoscopy tower & scope OEMs · ambulatory surgery centers

03 · LIVER MRIPROTOTYPE

LiverProfile AI — non-invasive liver assessment

Volumetric segmentation and staging of chronic liver disease from multiparametric MRI, built on CirrMRI600+, with calibrated uncertainty so borderline studies are flagged instead of silently scored. Delivered through VoxPixel, our end-to-end deployment framework — DICOM in, structured report out.

FOR: radiology groups · hepatology clinics · trial imaging endpoints

04 · DENTAL IMAGINGDATASET RELEASE

DentiAsk — radiograph question answering

A vision-language system for panoramic and periapical radiographs that answers clinical questions and drafts structured findings, backed by a purpose-built annotated VQA dataset. Aimed at the volume problem in general dentistry: many images, thin radiology support, wide reader-to-reader variance.

FOR: dental service organizations · imaging software vendors

05 · LOW-DOSE CTRESEARCH PREVIEW

CTDenoiser — diagnostic quality at reduced dose

One-step generative denoising for low-dose CT, benchmarked against established reconstruction and denoising baselines and scored by task-based detectability rather than PSNR alone. Designed to drop into existing reconstruction pipelines without retraining on site data.

FOR: CT OEMs · imaging centers under dose-reduction mandates

06 · DIGITAL PATHOLOGYRESEARCH PREVIEW

Training-free tissue classification

Retrieval-based classification over frozen self-supervised features with confidence-filtered abstention: a lab supplies a small reference gallery instead of thousands of annotated slides. Removes the annotation bottleneck that stalls most pathology AI pilots before they start.

FOR: pathology labs · CRO and biobank operations

RESEARCH USE ONLY — none of the above are FDA cleared or intended for diagnostic use.
