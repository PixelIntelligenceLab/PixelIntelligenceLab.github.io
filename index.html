(0008,1030) STUDY — CAPABILITIES

What we do

Four tightly connected practices, built on a decade of peer-reviewed medical imaging research.

01 · DATA

Curated medical imaging datasets

Multi-center, expertly annotated datasets for colonoscopy, capsule endoscopy, and liver MRI — designed for generalizability, with documented annotation protocols and inter-reader analysis.

02 · MODELS

Segmentation & detection architectures

State-of-the-art medical image segmentation models — from real-time polyp detection to transformer and state-space architectures for organ and lesion segmentation in CT and MRI.

03 · VALIDATION

Clinical AI validation & benchmarking

Rigorous, statistically grounded evaluation: out-of-distribution testing, cross-scanner generalization, uncertainty quantification, and diagnostic accuracy studies built for regulatory-grade evidence.

04 · ADVISORY

Research consulting & collaboration

Advisory for healthcare AI teams, dataset strategy for medtech companies, and academic–industry collaborations spanning grant-funded research to product-ready prototypes.

Pixel Intelligence Lab grows out of a decade of research at the intersection of computer vision and clinical medicine. Its founder has developed some of the most widely used open datasets and architectures in gastrointestinal imaging — including Kvasir-SEG, PolypDB, GastroVision, and CirrMRI600+ — alongside segmentation models such as ResUNet++, DoubleUNet, ColonSegNet, and TransNetR.

The lab's mission is simple: close the gap between what medical imaging AI achieves in papers and what it delivers at the bedside. That means multi-center data, honest out-of-distribution evaluation, and models designed for the realities of clinical deployment.
